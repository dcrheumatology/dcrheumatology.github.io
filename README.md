## 📋 Project Overview

This is a professional, SEO-optimized website for Dr. Tahereh Jamshidi, a board-certified rheumatologist practicing at Georgetown University Hospital and DC VA Medical Center in Washington, DC.

The website is designed to:
- ✅ Maximize search engine visibility
- ✅ Attract new patients
- ✅ Establish professional credibility
- ✅ Provide comprehensive information about services
- ✅ Rank highly for relevant medical searches

- **Schema.org Physician markup** with full credentials
- **Local Business schema** for both practice locations
- **sameAs links** to all major medical directories
- **Optimized meta tags** and title structure
- **Mobile-first responsive design**
- **Fast loading** with minimal dependencies
- **XML sitemap** for search engines

### Content Strategy
- **Name variations** (Tahereh and Tara) for search coverage

### Design
- **Modern, professional aesthetic**
- **Trust-building blue color scheme**
- **High accessibility standards**
- **Mobile-responsive** on all devices
- **Clear call-to-actions**
- **Professional photo integration**
